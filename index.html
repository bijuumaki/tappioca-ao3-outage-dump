A clean start

Four things were kept from the old site: the paper texture, the theme toggle, the Aa panel (text size and font) and reading progress. Try them: change the theme, open Aa, then scroll this page and reload. It remembers how far you got.

This is a sample passage standing in for a chapter. Reading progress is saved in this browser only, with no accounts. It keeps the furthest point reached, so scrolling back up never loses your place.
